# AI Use Disclosure

![AI Use Disclosure](AI_DISCLOSURE.png)

Artificial intelligence is now built into many common tools, including spellcheck, autocomplete, search, writing assistants, coding assistants, and development environments.

AI use is not simply present or absent. This disclosure rates four types of involvement so readers can see where AI contributed and where the work remained human-directed.

## How to Read the Ratings

All four categories use the same direction:

- **1/5:** Minimal AI involvement
- **5/5:** AI-led involvement

Each category is rated independently. The scores should not be averaged into a single overall rating.

## Rating Summary

| Category | Rating |
|---|---:|
| Planning and research | **__/5** |
| Content or code generation | **__/5** |
| Editing, debugging, and refinement | **__/5** |
| Human oversight and Independent verification | **__/5** |

> **AI use:** Planning __/5 · Generation __/5 · Refinement __/5 · Human Oversight __/5

# Rating Guide

## 1. Planning and Research

Includes brainstorming, outlines, source discovery, research summaries, project architecture, tool selection, and implementation planning.

| Level | Label | Description |
|---:|---|---|
| **1** | Minimal | AI provided isolated suggestions, definitions, or search terms. |
| **2** | Advisory | AI suggested several ideas, sources, tools, or possible approaches. |
| **3** | Collaborative | AI helped develop major sections of the outline, research plan, or technical approach. |
| **4** | Extensive | AI created most of the outline, research synthesis, or implementation plan. |
| **5** | AI-led | AI largely determined the direction, structure, and approach. |

## 2. Content or Code Generation

Includes sentences, paragraphs, documentation, images, functions, queries, tests, scripts, notebooks, and configuration files.

| Level | Label | Description |
|---:|---|---|
| **1** | Minimal | AI use was limited to spellcheck, autocomplete, syntax help, or isolated words and lines. |
| **2** | Advisory | AI generated small examples, passages, or code snippets incorporated into human-created work. |
| **3** | Collaborative | AI generated meaningful portions that were substantially revised or expanded by the human. |
| **4** | Extensive | AI produced most of the initial draft or implementation, followed by human revision. |
| **5** | AI-led | Most of the final writing, code, or other content remained substantially AI-generated. |

## 3. Editing, Debugging, and Refinement

Includes grammar correction, rewriting, restructuring, debugging, refactoring, documentation, optimization, and presentation improvements.

| Level | Label | Description |
|---:|---|---|
| **1** | Minimal | AI provided minor corrections, formatting help, or basic error explanations. |
| **2** | Advisory | AI suggested localized improvements, rewrites, or possible fixes. |
| **3** | Collaborative | AI revised, debugged, or refactored multiple meaningful sections. |
| **4** | Extensive | AI performed substantial rewriting, debugging, refactoring, or restructuring across most of the work. |
| **5** | AI-led | AI directed most revisions and produced most of the final form. |

## 4. Human oversight and Independent verification

Measures how much the author relied on AI to check facts, sources, logic, tests, outputs, and final quality. A higher score means **more reliance on AI and less independent human verification**.

| Level | Label | Description |
|---:|---|---|
| **1** | Minimal | The human independently reviewed, tested, and verified all important work. |
| **2** | Advisory | AI suggested checks or tests, but the human performed and evaluated them. |
| **3** | Collaborative | AI generated or performed meaningful checks, while the human independently verified major claims, logic, and outputs. |
| **4** | Extensive | AI performed most checking, testing, or fact verification, with limited independent human review. |
| **5** | AI-led | The final work was accepted largely based on AI review, with minimal independent testing, source verification, or demonstrated understanding. |
